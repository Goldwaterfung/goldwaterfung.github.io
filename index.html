4. Chinese Traditional Erhu Live: AI Motion Capture & Audiovisual Synchronization

Role: Systems Integration Engineer Organization: National Tainan University of the Arts

Background and Challenges

Deploying a real-time virtual avatar for live traditional Chinese Erhu performances presented substantial latency and synchronization challenges. Rapid bowing gestures combined with live audio streams created visual jitter and desync when processed through AI vision pose estimation models during rehearsals.

Cross-Domain Translation and Engineering Objectives

My objective was to determine an optimal buffer window between bowing pose estimation and continuous audio streams without compromising sound fidelity, delivering stable live performance synchronization.

Rehearsal Testing and Engineering Trade-Offs

  • Bridging Artistic Phrasing with Technical Metrics: While engineers focused on raw packet latency, the musician prioritized visual cadence and attack timing. By observing rehearsal sessions with curiosity, I mapped the musician's natural ~0.8s preparatory bowing cadence into frame-accurate buffer thresholds.
  • Collaborative Buffer Implementation: Recording motion tracking data alongside audio waveforms, we established a 1.5s deterministic streaming buffer that absorbed AI inference variance and eliminated visual snapping while preserving acoustic clarity.
  • Live Stage Validation: Conducted multiple full-dress simulations on stage to verify that virtual avatar responsiveness aligned with the musician's physical performance timing.

Product Impact and Practical Outcomes

The 1.5s deterministic buffer stabilized the vision tracking stream and maintained tight alignment with live acoustic audio throughout the university concert hall performance.

Stratawright - Digital Audio Workstation Software Built from Scratch for Agent CLI Workflows

An open-source C++20 digital audio workstation software built from scratch, featuring a signal architecture that allows autonomous coding agents to use CLI commands to inspect track telemetry and control multi-track audio workflows.

Why did I initiate this project? While AI agents excel at code and text automation, audio engineering remains locked in manual GUI interactions. When working on large multitrack sessions, creators spend substantial time on repetitive mechanical routing, gain staging, and track management. To explore how agents could automate audio workflows, I set out to bridge the gap between low-level audio signals and agentic CLI tooling.
What problem does this project solve? Constructing an audio workstation architecture natively supporting AI Agent CLI workflows. The system translates low-level audio signals (peak dB, LUFS loudness, spectral energy distribution, and waveform characteristics) into deterministic CLI protocols and tool-calling schemas. It enables AI coding agents (such as Claude Code, Cursor, or Gemini) to inspect audio telemetry, organize track groups, balance gain stages, adjust stereo panning, and automate DSP parameters through command-line execution.

2026

Measuring Visual Capture in Virtual Reality under Sustained Audiovisual Conflict with Third-Order Ambisonics

Investigating human cross-modal perception under audiovisual conflict in VR to provide empirical foundations for spatial computing interfaces.

Why did I conduct this research? As spatial computing and smart glasses advance, virtual visual and auditory cues are overlaid onto physical space. I wanted to understand the empirical limits of human perception when sensory inputs contradict: if high-fidelity spatial audio and visual stimuli deliver conflicting spatial locations, does the brain prioritize vision, and what is the measurable cognitive cost?

Three Key Experimental and Academic Contributions:

  1. Experimental Paradigm Innovation: Designed a decoupled head-gaze and hand-pointing tracking paradigm in Unity to isolate sensory capture and track real-time cognitive correction paths.
  2. Empirical Evidence of Sensory Capture and Cognitive Cost: Demonstrated that even with 16-channel Third-Order Ambisonics spatial audio, salient visual distractors dominate initial localization, with cross-modal conflict increasing cognitive latency by 1.6 seconds.
  3. Foundations for Spatial Computing: Grounded in cognitive psychology, proved that spatial audio alone cannot compensate for visual misalignment, establishing empirical guidelines for cross-modal coherence in spatial interfaces.
