Interactive 3D and Operational Tooling

In-store 3D previews that cut consultation time 35 percent

Designed and built a bespoke Unity C# preview application deployed on Android tablets with Google Cloud Storage asset streaming, resolving consultation ambiguity in a fast-turnaround salon.

Role Co-Founder & Product Engineer
Company MRKE Ltd.
Timeline 2022 - 2026
Key Systems Unity C#, Android, GCS Streaming

Context and Shop Floor Problem

MRKE operated as a physical fast-turnaround haircut business serving a community of 10 residential blocks. As co-founder, I managed unit economics, storefront operations, and service workflows.

Direct floor observation revealed a continuous operational bottleneck: flat 2D catalogs left customers unable to visualize volume, layering, and neckline tapers. Consultations dragged, creating customer anxiety and breaking our fast turnaround service promise. In particular, children aged 6 to 12 and teenagers struggled to articulate haircut preferences to stylists.

Design Workflow and Software Delivery

To resolve this communication friction, I mapped consultation workflows before building an in-house software solution:

  • User Journey Mapping in Figma: Mapped consultation steps from greeting to style confirmation, designing wireframes and interaction flows tailored to fast-paced tablet touch input.
  • Bespoke Unity C# Development: Built a lightweight real-time 3D preview tool in Unity C# optimized for budget Android tablets mounted at styling stations.
  • Google Cloud Storage OTA Streaming: Architected over-the-air asset streaming pipelines via Google Cloud Storage, enabling continuous 3D hairstyle model updates without taking tablets out of service or disrupting daily salon operations.
  • Stylist Feedback Loops: Instituted daily tracking logs where stylists recorded customer style selections and consultation satisfaction, using real operational data to refine 3D models and UI responsiveness.

Practical Outcomes and Business Impact

Measurable Result

Customers and stylists rotated 3D models 360 degrees before cutting. Consultation ambiguity and discussion time fell approximately 35 percent across hundreds of live sessions.

The 3D tool transformed indecisive consultations into confident agreements within seconds. Floor observation proved that the most enthusiastic users were youth customers, validating the decision to prioritize dedicated youth styles. The project confirmed that software built to solve frontline operational bottlenecks creates immediate commercial utility.
